About Everidemy

We're building the operating system for African schools.

Every day, school administrators across Africa spend hours on tasks that should take minutes — tracking attendance on paper, chasing fee payments, generating report cards by hand.

Everidemy was built to change that. We give schools the same modern tools that businesses have had for years — student management, online payments, analytics — at a price that works for schools of any size.

We believe every school, no matter how small, deserves technology that respects their time, protects their data, and helps them focus on what matters: teaching.
